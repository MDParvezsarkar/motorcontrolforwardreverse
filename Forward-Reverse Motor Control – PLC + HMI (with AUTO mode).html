Forward-Reverse Motor Control — PLC + Push-button + HMI

Press FWD / REV / STOP, or AUTO to run the belt back and forth by itself (operator panel or HMI).

① Ready

Supply on, KM1 and KM2 both OFF. PLC waits for FWD, REV or STOP from the push-button or HMI.

② Forward run

PLC energizes KM1: L1→U, L2→V, L3→W. Rotating field turns the motor clockwise.

③ Interlock + dead time

Opposite button pressed: PLC drops the running contactor first, waits T1 = 2 s. KM1 + KM2 together would short L2 to L3!

④ Reverse run

PLC energizes KM2: two phases are swapped (V gets L3, W gets L2). Field turns the other way, so the motor reverses.

⑤ AUTO mode

AUTO bit M0.0 set: PLC runs forward to LS-F, stops, waits T1 = 2 s, runs reverse to LS-R, waits, and repeats. FWD / REV are locked; STOP or AUTO again ends the cycle.

🏭 Real example: Conveyor in a packing plant

Motor 2.2 kW, 400 V, In ≈ 4.9 A, moves cartons to the packing or the return side.

  • KM1, KM2: 9 A AC-3 contactors, with a mechanical interlock between them
  • Electrical interlock: NC aux contact of each contactor wired in the other coil circuit
  • MCCB 10 A (motor-rated), overload set 4.9 A
  • Limit switches LS-F / LS-R stop the belt at each end
  • Dead time 1–2 s avoids plugging current and mechanical shock
  • Same method: hoist, crane, lift, gate, mixer, lathe

Press FWD, then REV while running: watch the dead time, then the motor and belt reverse.
